What to prepare

To make sure your dog's stay is as comfortable as possible, please bring the following.

🍚

Food

Please bring enough of your dog's own food for the duration of the stay.

💊

Medication (if any)

If your dog is on any medication, please bring the full course with clear dosage instructions.

🩲

Dog Diaper (male dogs)

For male dogs, please bring enough dog diapers to cover the full length of the stay.

🦮

Please ensure your dog arrives with a properly fitted collar and leash.
